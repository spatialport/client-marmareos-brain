# ICP

Purpose: Ideal customer profile(s), buying triggers, objections and decision process.

## Accepted knowledge

### Geography (priority order)
1. **Monaco / Côte d'Azur, in French** — the priority ADV and prospecting market (Cap-Ferrat, Cap d'Antibes, Saint-Tropez, Cannes, Nice) (MASTER_PROMPT_MARMAREOS.md, 03-lead-generation-prospecting.md).
2. **Milan** — second paid market since Aug 2026: best traffic-per-euro (CPC €0.25–1.55 vs €3+ London/Miami), native "azienda italiana" credibility, ~2h domestic logistics from Arco, top-5 growing UHNWI market (campagna-milano-search-spec.md).
3. Then IT (Como, Porto Cervo, Rome, Turin) and EN markets (London, Geneva) (03-lead-generation-prospecting.md).

### Four prospecting ICPs, by acquisition priority (03-lead-generation-prospecting.md)
- **ICP-1 Developer/promoteur del lusso** — ultra-prime residential, branded residences, 5-star resorts; economic committente moving large stone volumes; triggers: new luxury site announced, permis de construire, finishing-package supplier selection.
- **ICP-2 Studi di architettura** — the specifier; speaks Marmareos's native technical language (CAD/DXF, tolerances); triggers: new prestige commission, hospitality competitions, project published on Architonic/Archiproducts/press.
- **ICP-3 Interior designer** — finishes and "materials you see"; incl. yacht interiors (lightweight marble differentiator); very active on Instagram (signal listening, formal contact via email/LinkedIn).
- **ICP-4 Contractor premium** — executes the projects, buys/coordinates materials; pain point: unreliable suppliers; often arrives via referral from ICP-1/2.

### Buyer personas from the brand book (BRAND-BOOK.md §04)
- **A. Privato esigente** (40–65, prestige property, high income) — trusts the expert; pain: suppliers who promise and don't deliver.
- **B. Architetto/Interior designer** — wants a partner, not a supplier: time certainty, off-standard materials, CAD/DXF, on-site surveys; pain: suppliers who slow down the site.
- **C. Impresa premium** — decides on track record and reliability; pain: suppliers who disappear after the order or deliver non-conforming material.
- **D. Contract/Hospitality** — luxury hotels, 5-star resorts, flagship, nautical: lightweight solutions, special finishes, confidentiality.

### How they buy
- Long sales cycle, high ticket, few high-value deals; timeliness in the supplier-selection phase is decisive (00-roadmap-strumenti-ai.md).
- Prospecting method: quality over volume — first batch ~70 qualified contacts, multi-touch email+LinkedIn sequences over 3-4 weeks, IT/EN/FR, GDPR-compliant (professional emails only, legitimate-interest basis `[legal validation pending]`) (03-lead-generation-prospecting.md).
- 2026 specification trends favoring Marmareos: warm neutrals/travertines, green marbles, Calacatta/Statuario in hospitality, large slabs/bookmatch, tactile finishes, traceability, lightweight marble in nautical (DOSSIER.md §1).
