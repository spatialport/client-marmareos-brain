# Offer

Purpose: What the company sells, how it is packaged and priced, and what is out of scope.

## Accepted knowledge

### What Marmareos sells
Not slabs off a shelf: a **bespoke technical partner for the project**, from laser survey to delivery, with measurable quality. Six sellable offer lines (04-offerta-servizi-segmenti.md):

- **A. Selezione & materia** — material consulting, rapid sampling, lot-by-lot selection, access to rare marbles (greens, onyx, travertines). "La pietra giusta, scelta lotto per lotto — anche fuori dalla vetrina."
- **B. Progettazione tecnica** — on-site laser dimensional survey, executive CAD/CAM design, CAD/DXF support to studios.
- **C. Lavorazione su misura** — 5-axis CNC (Donatoni, JM Davidson, Prussiani) + artisan mastery, special finishes. The common denominator across all segments.
- **D. Grandi lastre & bookmatch** — full-height large formats, bookmatch, architectural cladding, facades (aligned with 2026 specification trends).
- **E. Marmo alleggerito** — natural slab on honeycomb panel where weight is a constraint (yacht/jet). "Il peso della pietra, non il suo vincolo."
- **F. Posa & continuità** — site coordination, protected logistics, certified global deliveries, after-sales (treatments, maintenance, restoration).
