# Positioning

Purpose: The market frame, the difference that matters, and the message hierarchy.

## Proposed knowledge (awaiting review by Alex)

### Positioning statement (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md)
> "Per architetti, interior designer e developer che firmano progetti d'eccezione tra Monaco e la Costa Azzurra, Marbles Marmareos è la boutique italiana della pietra naturale che garantisce qualità misurabile e un unico interlocutore tecnico dall'idea alla posa — perché unisce maestria artigianale dal 1972 e controllo numerico, con tolleranze documentate e tempi certi."

Claim sintetico: *"La pietra giusta, misurata fino al millimetro."* Brand statement: **"La Boutique Italiana delle Pietre Naturali"** (BRAND-BOOK.md). Guiding claim in EN: *"Measurable quality, from selection to delivery."* (BRAND-GUIDE.md). Note: public copy now avoids promising "posa/installation" (see offer.md conflict; CHECKLIST-GO-LIVE.md).

### The space occupied
Correction vs earlier draft: the positioning is **not** "curator of rare marbles from the Verona district" — it is **the bespoke technical partner with proof of measurable quality, speaking the designer's language**. Rarity is table stakes; measure/process/relationship is the differentiating axis (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md). Three deliberate choices: don't compete on scale/catalog (Antolini, Margraf); don't be a product design house (Salvatori, Citco); own the vacant "quality as proof" territory (laser surveys, documented tolerances) that almost nobody communicates (DOSSIER.md §2).

### Four message pillars (02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md)
1. **Artigianalità + tecnologia** — "Mano e macchina, insieme." Proof: 5-axis CNC + craft since 1972, laser survey → CAD/CAM.
2. **Qualità reale, misurabile** — "La qualità non si promette: si misura." Proof: millimetric tolerances `[2-3 publishable metrics pending from client]`. Strongest, least-contested differentiator.
3. **Esperienza premium su misura** — "Una boutique, non un catalogo." Proof: consultancy from first call, record sampling times, 4h first response / 48h quote SLAs, discretion.
4. **Interlocutore tecnico dei progettisti** — "Un unico interlocutore. Parliamo la lingua del progetto." Removes handoff risk between supply, fabrication and site.

### Competitive map (DOSSIER.md, 02-posizionamento-differenziazione.md)
- Italian high-end benchmarks: **Antolini** (1956, Verona — 1,000+ stones, scale/rarity), **Margraf** (1906, Vicenza — own quarries, heritage), **Salvatori** (1946 — product design house, Tom Ford/Armani), **Citco** (artistic marble). Gap they leave: 1:1 relationship, end-to-end technical service, certain lead times.
- Direct local competitors Monaco/Côte d'Azur: **Carat Diffusion** (Monaco, 2014 — "fournisseur exclusif en marbre rare à Monaco"; residential/commercial/yacht; also offers honeycomb lightweight marble and backlit slabs; showroom in Monaco) = competitor n.1, nearly overlapping positioning, dedicated dossier in _research/competitor-carat-diffusion.md. Others: Mdlc Select, La Pierre et le Marbre, Marbrerie de la Madeleine, Atelier de la pierre vençoise/Oddoart, Marbre Import.
- Caution: lightweight marble must NOT be communicated as a technical exclusive (Carat Diffusion has it too) — communicate assumed competence + measurable quality (04-offerta-servizi-segmenti.md).

### Copy governance
- Forbidden: scarcity rhetoric ("pochi progetti"), AI-sounding patterns ("Non è X. È Y.", empty superlatives, motivational closers), self-celebration without proof; targets presented as minimums to exceed (STYLE-COPY-GUIDE.md, directives approved by Alex 2026-06-10).
- Standard signature line across the package: *"La stessa precisione. Dalla pietra alla crescita."* (STYLE-COPY-GUIDE.md).
