- Self-definition: **"la boutique italiana delle pietre naturali"** — a boutique, not an industrial distributor. EN: "The Italian Boutique of Natural Stone"; FR: "La Boutique Italienne des Pierres Naturelles" (BRAND-BOOK.md).

- Website: **marblesmarmareos.it** — a well-made existing site built on **Lovable (React SPA, client-side rendered)**; rule of engagement: the site is never rebuilt, only extended (tracking, landings, privacy) (MASTER_PROMPT_MARMAREOS.md, seo-audit-strategia-2026-08-15.md).

### Mission, vision, values
- Mission: integrate Italian craftsmanship and advanced technology to deliver consulting, design and made-to-measure stonework with certain lead times, rigorous QC and full project support (BRAND-BOOK.md).
- Vision: be the most reliable Italian partner for architects, designers and developers for high-end natural stone (BRAND-BOOK.md).
- 6 core values: Artigianalità · Eccellenza della Materia · Dal Rilievo al Progetto Finito · Relazione & Discrezione · Velocità senza Compromessi · Sostenibilità Consapevole (BRAND-BOOK.md).
- Internal culture ("Company OS"): the **3P rule — Precisa · Puntuale · Personale**; decision test "Se non lo direi al cliente, non lo faccio"; quality before margin (BRAND-BOOK.md).
- Service standards: first reply ≤ 4 working hours; quote ≤ 48 working hours; order confirmation same day; post-delivery follow-up ≤ 30 days (BRAND-BOOK.md).

### Capabilities & signature projects
- Production: laser dimensional survey on site → executive CAD/CAM design → **5-axis CNC** (machines: Donatoni Jet, JM Davidson, Prussiani Cut-Jet, Prussiani Mirror) + artisan finishing; **lightweight marble on honeycomb panel** for yacht/jet interiors; 8 surface finishes (BRAND-BOOK.md).
- Reference projects (Monaco): **Mareterra, Hôtel Hermitage, Monte Carlo Bay, Residenza Montecarlo, Private Offices** — all usable in PR/ads only `[with client authorization, pending]` (MASTER_PROMPT_MARMAREOS.md, BRAND-BOOK.md).
